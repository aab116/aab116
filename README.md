## About me

I'm a Software Systems Engineering student at the University of Regina, graduating in May 2028. I build full-stack apps backed by real databases, with tests and AI features, and I also work on embedded hardware with STM32 boards and FPGAs.

I'm currently working in SCADA and automation, and I'm trying to bring computer vision into manufacturing for quality control.

At school, my capstone team is building Nuturaa, a food price forecasting tool for food banks, hospitals and other organizations that buy food in bulk. I'm looking for co-op and internship roles in software engineering.
## Projects

| Project | What it does | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nuturaa (Capstone, in progress) | Forecasts food prices from Statistics Canada data and a buyer's own purchase history, then gives a buy now, wait or monitor signal for each item with 30, 60 and 90-day outlooks. Also includes a marketplace where Saskatchewan producers sell surplus food directly to buyers | React, Vite, Express, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker |
| Engineering Olympics | Party game for Engineering Multicultural Night that handles 50+ players at once. One big screen runs the show and everyone plays on their phone, through a Royal Rumble, trivia, bridge building and drawing rounds | Node.js, WebSockets |
| [UR Lost & Found](https://github.com/aab116/Lost-and-Found) | Lost-property tracker for University of Regina staff and security, with 4 staff workflows and 2 reporting views | Node.js, PostgreSQL, EJS |
| [URMarketplace](https://github.com/aab116/URmarketplace) | Campus marketplace prototype built with the V-Model SDLC, with JUnit tests on the core flows | Java, Maven, SQLite, Docker, JUnit |
| [Stranger Things Light Communication System](https://github.com/aab116/StrangerThings-Light-Communication-System) | Types on a PS/2 keyboard and lights up the matching letter on a 26-LED wall, with interrupts and PWM audio. Took 2nd place at Lab Legends | STM32, C |
| [Mastermind Game](https://github.com/aab116/Mastermind-Game-) | Mastermind on an STM32 board. LEDs show digit and position hints, driven by a state machine | STM32, C |
| [4-Bit Calculator](https://github.com/aab116/4-Bit-Calculator-BASYS-Board) | Adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides on an FPGA, with FSM control, a 7-segment display and error handling | VHDL, Basys 3 |
